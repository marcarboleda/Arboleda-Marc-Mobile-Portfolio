01 Undergraduate thesis · De La Salle Lipa

Tabula Rasa

Game UI · Research data · 2026

Our thesis is a candidate for Best Thesis.

Study recruitment story

A no-code game that teaches reinforcement learning. Players build a creature, tune its rewards with sliders, and watch it learn to walk on their own device, then send it into raids and live races.

Role
Design & data analysis
Team
Team of 3

Reinforcement learning is one of the hardest parts of AI to learn, and most tools that teach it sit behind Python, math and training logs. Tabula Rasa turns students into "AI trainers." They assemble a creature from body parts, set twelve reward and penalty values with sliders, and watch it learn to walk through a PPO engine that runs offline on an iPhone or Mac. Trained agents compete in server-validated raids and live races, so good reward design has visible consequences.

My part was the design and the research data: the look and feel of the game and its study materials, collecting data from participants, and analyzing the results.

What I did

  • Designed the interface and visual style so a technical topic feels playful and approachable.
  • Ran data gathering with 38 college and Grade 12 STEM students at De La Salle Lipa: a 25-item pretest and posttest, and a 31-item user acceptance test adapted from MEEGA+.
  • Analyzed learning gains. Mean scores rose from 15.49 to 18.84 out of 25 (n = 37): paired t(36) = 5.83, p < .001, dz = 0.96, confirmed by a Wilcoxon signed-rank test. All four concept areas improved by 10 to 18 percentage points.
  • Analyzed user acceptance. The overall rating was 3.29 out of 4 (Strongly Agree), with Fun rated highest at 3.59, and the questionnaire was highly consistent (Cronbach's α = .98).

02 D.H. Magpantay Dairy Farm · proposed system

Dairy Farm Management System

UI design · Dashboard · 2025

A command center for a working dairy farm: herd, calving, milk output and medicine stock on one screen, with alerts before anything runs out or expires.

Role
UI designer
Team
With a developer friend

A dairy farm runs on records that change every day: which animals are due to calve, how much milk comes in, which medicines are running low. For D.H. Magpantay Dairy Farm's proposed system, I designed an interface that puts all of it in one place and shows staff what needs attention first. A developer friend built it; the interface is my work.

What I did

  • Designed five core screens across two modules, Records and Inventory: Dashboard, Animal Inventory, Medicine Inventory, Calving Records and Milk Production.
  • Built the screens on one design system: forest-green navigation, a shared table style, and search, filter and add actions always in the same spot, so staff learn the layout once.
  • Designed the dashboard around action, not just data: reminders, expiry and low-stock alerts, upcoming calving and herd status come first.

03 Musikalista · Statistics Officer

Membership Database

Data visualization · Google Sheets · 2026

Walkthrough, shown with 50 fictional members

A flat member list turned into a live dashboard: instant charts, clean data entry, and one search box that finds anyone by any field.

Role
Database designer
Team
Solo

As Statistics Officer of Musikalista, I'm responsible for the org's membership data. I rebuilt our member list into a small database with a live dashboard, so officers can read the whole membership at a glance instead of scrolling through rows.

What I did

  • Built a dashboard with four live charts (department, year level, music roles and genres) that update as members are added.
  • Set up validation with dropdowns and date checks, so bad data is stopped at entry instead of cleaned up later.
  • Wrote one dynamic formula that powers search across every field, including birth month for birthday greetings.
  • Kept privacy first: this demo uses 50 fictional members, and real member data stays within the org.
